How Cleveland Homeowners Can Extend HVAC Life

Most mid-season breakdowns trace back to maintenance that never happened, and the habits that prevent them are easy. Swap your filter every one to three months, keep plants and shrubs at least two feet from the outdoor condenser, and hose off the condenser coil every spring. Then book a professional tune-up each spring and fall, one for cooling and one for heating, so our technicians can catch small problems while they're still cheap to fix.

How old is your system? Its age says a lot about what comes next. Most well-maintained air conditioners reach 12 to 15 years, furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15. If your system is beyond that range and is also showing rising bills, rooms that never quite reach temperature, or frequent repairs, it's usually due for a planned replacement, which is almost always cheaper and less stressful than a rushed emergency install in peak season.

Small problems rarely stay small when they're ignored. Take a weak capacitor: replacing it is a short, inexpensive repair, but if it's ignored for a season, it can take the compressor with it, and just like that a minor fix has become a full system replacement. That's why we ask homeowners to save our number in their phone and call at the first hint of trouble. Catching it early saves time and money and prevents a miserable night.
